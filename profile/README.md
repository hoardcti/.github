# Hoard CTI

**Open-source, autonomous cyber threat intelligence, free for everyone.**

Hoard CTI gathers threat intelligence from across the internet, enriches it, and publishes it as a free, continuously updated feed. It runs autonomously with no manual curation, and its modular architecture keeps each collector and processing stage independent, making the platform easy to maintain and easy to extend.

It's built for anyone, from solo analysts and students to SOC teams and large organisations. The feed is free to use, with little to no limits.
